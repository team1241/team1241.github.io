Team 1241 — Resources

FRC Team 1241 · notes & interactive lessons

  • Lesson 01 · start here Boxes, Arrows, Circles — Reading Block Diagrams The three-symbol picture language every control system is drawn in — signals, blocks, summing junctions, and open vs. closed loops.
  • Lesson 02 Close the Loop — Feedback & PID Control Measure, compare, correct: tune P, I, and D yourself on a live simulated elevator and learn what each term is actually for.
  • Lesson 03 Push First, Correct Later — Feedforward Control Model the mechanism, predict the push it needs, and command it before any error exists — flywheels, arms, elevators, and WPILib's kS·kV·kA.
  • Lesson 04 Four Wheels, One Motion — Kinematics & Swerve The geometry dictionary between "the business end goes here" and "each joint does this" — forward and inverse kinematics, from a two-joint arm to a full swerve model, skew and all.
  • Lesson 05 Count Your Steps — Odometry Tracking pose by adding up every wheel step — encoders, gyros, drift, and why the bookkeeping eventually needs outside help.
  • Lesson 06 Where Am I? — Sensor Fusion & Kalman Filters How a robot combines dead reckoning and noisy absolute measurements into one confident position estimate.
